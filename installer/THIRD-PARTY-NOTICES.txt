  https://ffmpeg.org  ·  https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/  ·  source: https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg
  Downloaded from https://github.com/GyanD/codexffmpeg and checked against a fixed SHA-256 before use.

Downloaded components are stored in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Konbini\deps (or "Konbini Data\deps" in the portable
version) and can be removed from Settings → Components.
